# Continuity, Knowledge Tracking & State Architecture
# Folytonosság, tudásáramlás és állapotkezelés

This reference establishes rigorous methods for tracking information flow, object custody, physical limitations, and commitments across scenes and chapters.

---

## 1. Information Has a Date and a Route (A tudás útvonala)

Characters cannot act on facts merely because the author or reader knows them. Every piece of knowledge requires a legitimate transfer route (eyewitness observation, message, overheard remark, or supported deduction).

### The Five Discrete Epistemic States:

| Epistemic State | Definition | Narrative Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Observation** | Character witnessed the event with their own senses. | High certainty, but perspective limits what was visible. |
| **Testimony / Message** | Character heard it from another or read it in a document. | Subject to the informant's bias, omission, or deception. |
| **Suspicion / Deduction** | Character noticed an anomaly and inferred a hypothesis. | Unconfirmed; can lead to catastrophic missteps. |
| **Mistaken Belief** | Character is convinced of an account that is factually false. | Drives tragic or ironic choices; cannot be easily talked away. |
| **Reader-Only Knowledge** | Known to the reader via another POV, concealed from this character. | Dramatic irony; creates suspense through anticipation. |

> **Chronological Invariance Rule:** A subsequent revelation can never retroactively alter what a character knew or said in an earlier scene.

## 2. Object Custody, Bodies & Environmental Constraints
## Tárgybirtoklás, testek és fizikai korlátok

Physical constraints survive scene transitions:

1. **Custody vs. Ownership:**
   - Possessing an object, having permission to use it, and owning it are distinct states.
   - Setting a key on a table is an offer, not a completed handoff until picked up.
2. **Body States and Injuries:**
   - Exhaustion, blisters, hypothermia, fever, or intoxication do not vanish when romance or danger begins.
   - Physical trauma imposes measurable limitations on mobility, perception, and emotional patience.
3. **Environmental Logistics:**
   - Impassable snowdrifts, broken bridges, depleted batteries, and severed communication lines remain blocked until concrete physical work clears them.

## 3. Commitments, Promises & State Changes
## Megállapodások és kötelezettségek

Words spoken between characters constitute binding state changes:
- **Offer vs. Acceptance:** A proposed bargain does not bind the other party until accepted. A counter-offer invalidates the initial premise.
- **Conditional Promises:** A character cannot fulfill a promise while violating its agreed condition without paying an interpersonal price.
- **Unresolved Debts:** Keep outstanding obligations visible; they generate natural dramatic leverage in negotiations.
